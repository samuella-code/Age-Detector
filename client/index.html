How It Works

Three simple steps to instant, privacy-focused facial age estimation.

STEP 01

1. Upload a Photo

Select a clear image containing a visible face. We accept all standard image formats including JPG, PNG, and WebP.

STEP 02

2. AI Analyzes the Image

The model examines facial features and biometric landmarks to estimate apparent age in fractions of a second.

STEP 03

3. View Your Result

See the estimated age in a simple result card complete with confidence interval metrics and age range distributions.

About

Age Detector is a privacy-first AI tool that estimates apparent age from facial images entirely on your local machine. No images are stored, sent to third-party servers, or used for model training. Your data stays yours.

Powered by deep convolutional neural networks trained on diverse datasets, the model achieves high accuracy across age ranges and lighting conditions — all in sub-second inference time.
